# Current scope

The detector uses the EUMETNET OPERA DBZH maximum-reflectivity composite as its sole primary observation source for Sofia. Craiova's OPERA volume is retained as an experimental vertical-profile context source.

Send one Telegram **Severe Reflectivity Alert** per tracked storm when a fresh, confirmed OPERA track has a maximum observed band of at least 65 dBZ and its medium-or-higher confidence trajectory enters the configured target radius within 30 minutes. Do not mutate alert state or send a message from stale or incomplete data.

The system reports radar reflectivity signatures, not official meteorological warnings or confirmed hail.
